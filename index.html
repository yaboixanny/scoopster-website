What to Expect Before Your First Scoop

Before a first visit, we’ll ask which parts of the yard need scooping and how to reach them. A side run, a second lawn or an extra gate is worth mentioning when you request a price.

Choose a one-off catch-up if you’re restarting after a break. If your dog uses the space daily, ask us to compare weekly and alternate-week collection for your household.

Once the scope is agreed, you have a clear cleanup area and an entry arrangement. Keep pets inside for the visit and let us know when the gate or yard layout changes.
