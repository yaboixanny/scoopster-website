Pricing FAQs

Do you charge extra for multiple dogs?

We base pricing on yard size and waste volume. Two dogs don't double the price — we'll give you a fair quote.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Your quote is your price. No fuel surcharges, no service fees.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No contracts. Cancel or pause anytime with a quick message.

Do you offer discounts for prepayment?

Yes. Ask about monthly or seasonal prepay discounts.

What if I'm not happy with the service?

We'll make it right. If your yard wasn't cleaned to your satisfaction, we'll come back.

Is there a charge if I skip a week?

No. Just give us 24 hours notice and we'll skip your visit at no charge.

What to Include When Asking for an Exact Price

A useful estimate starts with the area that needs attention. If your dog only uses a fenced portion of a larger lot, describe that portion. If there are several dog runs, include each one rather than giving only the size of the main lawn.

Next, explain whether this is an ongoing arrangement or the first cleanup after a long break. Existing accumulation can make the initial job different from later routine scoops. Mention long grass, heavy leaves or limited access so we can establish the scope before booking.

When comparing costs, keep the interval in mind: a per-visit starting rate is different from a monthly total. Ask which visits the quote covers and whether an initial catch-up is priced separately. The calculator is a planning aid; your confirmed quote is the price to use when deciding.
