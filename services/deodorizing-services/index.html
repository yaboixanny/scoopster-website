A Treatment for Outdoor Pet Areas

Deodorizing is an optional addition to waste collection, rather than a replacement for scooping. Tell us where you notice the smell: a dog run, a section of lawn or another outdoor surface. The treatment needs to suit that location and the manufacturer’s directions.

An outdoor treatment is not a grooming spray. It should not be applied to your dog or used in place of bathing. If you’re asking about a particular product, request its name and label information so the intended use is clear.

What to Confirm Before Treatment

Product and Label

Ask which product will be used and how its label applies to your yard. Product-specific information is more useful than a blanket promise that every treatment is safe in every setting.

Pets and Children

Confirm any access restrictions, drying time or other precautions before the appointment. Follow the instructions supplied for the product instead of assuming the area can be used immediately.

Grass, Plants and Surfaces

Describe the area to be treated, including planting beds and hard surfaces. We need to discuss suitability before application; deodorizing does not promise lawn repair or fertilization.

Start With the Cleanup, Then Assess the Odour

We first remove visible waste from the agreed space. Let us know which spots continue to smell and when you notice it most. The amount of use, surface and current conditions help determine what to discuss for the visit.

Results and repeat-treatment needs depend on the job and product. We do not promise that one application will permanently eliminate odours or that a monthly schedule suits every property. Ask us to include the proposed treatment and any follow-up in your quote.

Continue ordinary pickup between visits. Deodorizing is not a claim that the yard has been disinfected or that all bacteria and parasites have been removed.

Yard Treatment Questions

What precautions apply around pets and children?

Ask for the product name, label directions and any drying or re-entry instructions before treatment. Keep pets and children away from the application area as directed for that product.

Is yard deodorizer the same as dog grooming spray?

No. This service concerns outdoor areas. Do not apply a yard-treatment product to your dog; its intended surfaces and uses must be checked on the label.

How often should a yard be deodorized?

The interval depends on the product, treated surface, use and odour problem. Discuss the need for repeat visits when requesting a quote rather than assuming monthly treatment is necessary.

Can treatment be used on my grass or plants?

Tell us about the lawn, planting beds and other surfaces before booking. Suitability depends on the chosen product and its label directions; we do not claim that treatment fertilizes or repairs a lawn.

Can deodorizing be added to waste collection?

Ask about including a treatment with an existing cleanup visit. We’ll confirm the scope, application requirements and any additional price before booking.
