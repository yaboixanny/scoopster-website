Frequently Asked Questions

What day will you come?

Tell us your preferred weekday when enquiring. Your confirmed day depends on your address and the service route; it is agreed before recurring visits begin.

What if I need to skip a week?

No problem. Just let us know 24 hours in advance. No fees, no hassle.

Do I need to be home?

You can be out during the appointment. Arrange an accessible gate and secure your dog before the visit so the weekly scoop can go ahead.

What happens in winter?

We scoop year-round. Snow and cold don't stop us — in fact, frozen poop is easier to pick up.
