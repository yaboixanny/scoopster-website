Dog Walks and Yard Care in Ancaster

Local Parks and Trails

For a wooded outing, the Tiffany Falls trail off Wilson Street East offers a waterfall walk. Longer outings can start from the Dundas Valley access on Sulphur Springs Road. Check the conservation authority’s visitor notices before heading out.

Hamilton’s Pickup Rules Apply Here Too

Carry bags on Wilson Street walks and around the Tiffany Falls approach. Hamilton’s Responsible Animal Ownership By-law 12-031, section 8.1, requires immediate waste removal away from your own premises, with service- and police-animal exceptions. Section 8.2 requires timely removal at home.

Scheduled yard cleanup helps with the home routine. Carry bags on walks and pick up between visits as needed. In City parks, keep dogs leashed outside designated off-leash areas and follow posted rules.
