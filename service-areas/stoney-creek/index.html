Dog Walks and Yard Care in Stoney Creek

Local Parks and Trails

Try the Battlefield Creek Trail from Battlefield Park on King Street East for a local trail walk. Near Winona, Fifty Point Conservation Area offers a lakeside outing; check its current pet-access rules before visiting.

Hamilton’s Pickup Rules Apply Here Too

Pick up on Battlefield walks and around Winona’s residential streets. Hamilton’s Responsible Animal Ownership By-law 12-031, section 8.1, requires immediate waste removal away from your own premises, with service- and police-animal exceptions. Section 8.2 requires timely removal at home.

Scheduled yard cleanup helps with the home routine. Carry bags on walks and pick up between visits as needed. In City parks, keep dogs leashed outside designated off-leash areas and follow posted rules.

Check the City’s dog-park rules and off-leash map.
